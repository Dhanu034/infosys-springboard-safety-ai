The problem, our solution, and what is new

Current industry reality

  • Fatal accidents, delayed detection. 1 in 5 worker fatalities occur in construction, and manual safety inspections miss 60%+ of real-time PPE violations.

  • Fragmented compliance. Safety manuals (OSHA, BOCW, ISO) are trapped in hundreds of PDF pages with no live verification.

  • Slow insurance claims. Post-incident investigations take weeks because there is no tamper-evident visual telemetry or supervisor audit trail.

BuildSure AI breakthrough

  • Autonomous multi-agent ecosystem. Site Risk, Safety, Compliance and Insurance agents collaborate in real time.

  • Deterministic multi-factor scoring. A mathematical risk pulse, P × I × E, plus a unified project risk score (0.35 × Safety + 0.35 × Compliance + 0.30 × Insurance) calculated per zone.

  • Instant audit trails. Automated supervisor resolution workflows and audit logs with zero cloud lock-in.

Two pillars: deep vision and modern web

Deep Neural Networks (DNN)

YOLOv8 30+ FPS Confidence calibration

YOLOv8 object detection. A fine-tuned model, trained on Hugging Face & Roboflow construction-safety datasets, inferencing at 30+ FPS across 10 classes (Hardhat, Safety Vest, Person, NO-Hardhat, NO-Safety Vest, Machinery, Vehicle and more).

Head & torso geometric spatial association

  • Upper 35% of the bounding box anchors helmet compliance.
  • Middle 40% anchors high-visibility vest compliance.

Confidence calibration. Eliminates false alarms by flagging uncertain detections as needs_human_review.

Modern Web Programming (MWP)

React 18 Vite 5 FastAPI SQLite Recharts pypdf + pytesseract JSON / CSV / PDF export

  • High-performance React 18 + Vite 5. Sub-second UI updates, a glassmorphic HUD design, Recharts visualizations and native print CSS.

  • Asynchronous FastAPI gateway. A high-concurrency Python backend on a Uvicorn ASGI server, with SQLAlchemy ORM streaming live telemetry and image inference.

  • Zero-paid-API architecture. 100% free local document extraction via pypdf, with a pytesseract OCR fallback.

  • Multi-format enterprise exports. Instant JSON export, standardized CSV generation and native print / save-to-PDF styling.
